## What changed and why

One change, in `convo/prompts.py` (sentence checker only): added two worked examples of **real** mistakes, each with an English explanation:
- 我想喝一块蛋糕。 → 我想吃一块蛋糕。 (vocabulary: 吃 for food, 喝 for drinks)
- 我要两杯三明治。 → 我要两个三明治。 (measure word: 杯 only for drinks)

Neither is a quiz sentence (unit test checks).

Why: v3 removed all false corrections (0/3) but became cautious. It missed 我想吃一杯咖啡 (36, 吃→喝) and 我要一杯面包 (01, wrong measure word), so the aim was to win those catches back.

## What I'd change next (v5), and why

Prompt examples alone swing between over-correcting (v4) and under-correcting (v3). Next single change: a **second check, in code**. When the checker flags a sentence, ask the model one short yes/no question: "Is this original sentence already correct, natural Chinese?" If it answers yes, keep the sentence unchanged. Separating "is it wrong?" from "how do I fix it?" should cut the false corrections (24, 27) while keeping v4's catches. Risk to watch: it may also drop some real corrections, so mistakes caught may fall.
