## What changed and why

One change, in `convo/prompts.py` (sentence checker only): added three worked examples of **correct** café sentences, each answered with `has_error: false` and the sentence unchanged (我们要结账。, 不要冰，谢谢。, 我们周末再来这家店吃饭。). None of them are quiz sentences; a unit test checks this.

Why: false corrections were 2/3 in both v1 and v2. v2's stronger written rule only changed *which* correct sentences got "fixed" (v2 changed 我要买单 → 我买单 and 我明天再来这里喝咖啡 → 我明天再来喝咖啡). Small models tend to copy examples better than they follow rules.

## What I'd change next (v4), and why

v3 has no false corrections on the tune split, but it has become cautious and still misses word-choice and measure-word mistakes (36 吃→喝, 01 一杯面包). Next single change: add one or two worked examples of a **real** word-choice or measure-word mistake (new sentences, not from the quiz), so the model sees that it should still fix those. Watch that false corrections stay at 0/3. After that, explanations in English (a small code check that asks again when an explanation is mostly Chinese) is the next most useful change.

Caveat: the tune split has only 3 correct sentences and 7 mistakes, so one sentence moves a score by 14–33 points. Treat small differences with caution.
