## What changed and why

One change, in `convo/review.py` (plus its prompt in `convo/prompts.py`): when the checker flags a sentence, the model is asked a **second, separate yes/no question**: "is this original sentence already correct, natural Chinese?". If it clearly says yes, the sentence is kept unchanged (`guard: verified_correct`). Any unclear answer keeps the checker's decision.

Why: v3 and v4 showed that prompt examples trade one failure for another. v3 had 0/3 false corrections but caught only 5/7. v4 caught 7/7 but wrongly "fixed" 2/3 correct sentences (24 不要糖 → 不要加糖, 27 我要买单 → 我要付钱). The idea was to separate "is it wrong?" from "how do I fix it?".

## What I'd change next (v6, the last version), and why

Keep the idea of a second check, but change how it asks. A sentence judged **on its own** gets "it's fine" too easily. v6 will show the model **both versions side by side** (A = what the learner wrote, B = the proposed fix) and ask which is more correct and natural, answering A if they're equally good. A wrong sentence next to its fix should lose clearly (04, 36, 40), while a correct sentence next to an unnecessary rewrite should be kept (24, 27).
